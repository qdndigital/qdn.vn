The pick: keep all three. Instrument Serif carries the premium/human voice (use it for the one italic word in a headline), Inter does all the heavy lifting (and the logo wordmark, ExtraBold), JetBrains Mono gives the engineered texture. QDN leans a touch more on the serif; QSortby leans on Inter + mono for SaaS clarity. One system, two personalities.
